Meteorium Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-08-19 / 日本語

Meteorium ("the App") is an astronomy visualisation app provided by Avellsky.

  • The App does not collect, transmit, or store any data, including personal information.
  • The App never initiates a connection on its own. Only when you use “Published data” does it contact the public data archive (GitHub) to download a data file. That request carries nothing about you.
  • Apart from that, the App makes no network connections: the orbital computation and the rendering all happen on your device.
  • Opening a link in the App (the manual, for example) hands off to your device's browser; anything that follows is the browser's traffic, not the App’s.
  • There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no accounts.
  • Preferences (such as display settings) are stored only on your device.
